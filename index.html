Privacy Policy

Still Leaf - Whole Plant Care • Surface Apps

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Still Leaf collects minimal information required to deliver your botanical sanctuary experience:

  • Account Credentials: Email address and display name upon sign-in.
  • Plant Photos & Care Notes: Photos you upload are stored securely in Cloudflare R2 object storage.
  • Usage & Billing Data: In-app purchase entitlement status verified via Google Play Services.

2. Zero Data-Selling Commitment

Surface Apps strictly values your privacy. We NEVER sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data, email address, or plant photos to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Encryption & Storage

Your plant photos and account data are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored securely in Cloudflare R2 storage.

4. User Control & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may delete individual plants or growth photos at any time within the app. To request complete account and data deletion, contact support@surfaceapps.com.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Surface Apps at support@surfaceapps.com.
